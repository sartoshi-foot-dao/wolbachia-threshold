# Prior work

What each paper shows, in our own words, and how it bears on the claim in [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md). "Checked" says how much was read: `full`, `abstract`, or `not checked`. First pass by Claude Fable 5.2 on 2026-11-14; anything marked `abstract` must be read in full before the paper relies on it.

## Closest to our claim

| Paper | What it shows | Bearing | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reuter, Lehmann & Guillaume 2008, *BMC Evol Biol* 8:134 | In an island model with fixed population per deme and a fecundity cost, subdivision helps cytoplasmic incompatibility invade. They attribute the opposite result of Wade & Stevens 1994 to global competition. Weak selection, rare invader, non-overlapping generations | Our container versus shared crowding contrast largely matches their local versus global regulation. Their mechanism is drift-generated variance between demes, not brood size | abstract |
| Wade & Stevens 1994 | Population structure impedes cytoplasmic incompatibility (as described by Reuter et al.) | The other side of the same contrast | not checked |
| Haygood & Turelli 2009, *Evolution* 63:432 | Evolution of incompatibility-inducing microbes in subdivided host populations | Must be read before any novelty claim | not checked (exists) |
| Freeland & McCabe 1997, *Heredity* 78:391 | Survivors of broods thinned by incompatibility gain only a small advantage | Matches our "failed eggs take room" result (1 point at most) | abstract |
