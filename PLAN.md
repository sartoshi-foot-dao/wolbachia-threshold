# Plan: from blog finding to preprint

**Target claim.** When larvae compete within each breeding container, and container-mates tend to share infection status, a Wolbachia cost paid in eggs counts for less than its face value, so the invasion threshold falls. A cost paid through adult survival gets no such discount.

**Target venue.** bioRxiv, then *Journal of Theoretical Biology* or *Theoretical Population Biology*. *American Naturalist* or *Proc B* only if the formula is clean and the *Aedes* case shows the effect matters in practice.

**Biggest risk.** The effect is already known in another form. Reuter, Lehmann & Guillaume (2008) showed that local population regulation helps cytoplasmic incompatibility invade. Step 1 decides whether there is a paper.
